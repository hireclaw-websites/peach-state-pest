Pest Library

Common Pests in Georgia

Know your enemy. Learn about the pests that threaten Georgia homes and how we handle them.

Subterranean Termites

Reticulitermes spp.

Georgia's most destructive pest, causing billions in damage annually. These silent destroyers feed on wood 24/7 and can compromise your home's structural integrity before you even know they're there.

Warning Signs:

  • Mud tubes on foundation walls
  • Hollow-sounding wood
  • Discarded wings near windows
  • Bubbling or peeling paint
Schedule Inspection

Rodents

Rattus & Mus species

Mice and rats are more than just a nuisance—they spread disease, contaminate food, and cause property damage by chewing through wires, insulation, and wood. One pair of mice can produce up to 60 offspring per year.

Warning Signs:

  • Droppings in cabinets or drawers
  • Gnaw marks on food packaging
  • Scratching sounds in walls
  • Nests made of shredded material
Schedule Inspection

Fire Ants

Solenopsis invicta

These aggressive invaders deliver painful stings and can be dangerous to children and pets. Fire ants build large mound nests and will swarm anything that disturbs their colony.

Warning Signs:

  • Dome-shaped mounds in yard
  • Swarming behavior when disturbed
  • Painful, burning stings
  • Multiple mounds appearing
Schedule Inspection

American Cockroaches

Periplaneta americana

Often called "palmetto bugs," these large roaches are common in Georgia. They spread bacteria, trigger allergies, and can contaminate food and surfaces in your home.

Warning Signs:

  • Live or dead roaches seen
  • Droppings that look like coffee grounds
  • Egg casings (oothecae)
  • Musty, oily odor
Schedule Inspection

Mosquitoes

Culicidae family

Georgia's warm, humid climate makes it a mosquito paradise. Beyond itchy bites, mosquitoes can transmit West Nile virus, Zika, and other diseases. Effective control requires treating both adult mosquitoes and breeding sites.

Breeding Sites:

  • Standing water in gutters
  • Bird baths and flower pots
  • Ponds and pools
  • Tire swings and children's toys
Schedule Inspection

Brown Recluse & Black Widow

Loxosceles & Latrodectus

Georgia is home to two venomous spiders that pose serious health risks. The brown recluse and black widow require professional treatment to ensure your home is safe for your family.

Warning Signs:

  • Irregular webs in dark corners
  • Spiders in closets or basements
  • Shed skins or egg sacs
  • Funnel-shaped webs (recluse)
